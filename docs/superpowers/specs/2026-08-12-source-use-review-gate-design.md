# Source-Use and Independent-Review Gate Design

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Status:** Approved for implementation under the user's standing instruction to proceed and self-review
**Scope:** Evidence capture, source-use gating, and offline independent-review preparation

## Objective

Turn the quarantined public-market intake into a defensible, fail-closed workflow. The workflow must distinguish public technical access from permission for the intended use, preserve dated evidence without copying whole legal pages into Git, and prepare genuinely independent human review without fabricating permission, identities, decisions, or adjudication.

The immediate real-world outcome is intentionally a blocked release: the existing 1,000 Polymarket candidates remain quarantined until a qualified human or source owner confirms that the exact retention and derived-use scope is permitted.

## Official-source finding

Polymarket's current official Predictions API overview identifies the Gamma API as the service for discovering events and markets and retrieving their metadata. The existing intake run also demonstrates technical access to the public market endpoint. Neither fact establishes a reuse license.

Polymarket's official institutional-data page separately says that capital-markets entities seeking to consume Polymarket data must consult Polymarket and ICE. Its scope expressly includes API and on-chain data as well as derived, aggregated, or anonymized data. Because the contemplated system includes proprietary automated-trading research, the conservative machine decision is `requires_external_confirmation`.

References:

- <https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/predictions/overview>
- <https://institutional.polymarket.com/>

This finding is a source-policy control, not legal advice and not a conclusion about the legal enforceability or geographic reach of any term.

## Non-goals

- No legal approval generated by software or by an AI model.
- No claim that a public or unauthenticated endpoint permits retention, model evaluation, redistribution, or commercial use.
- No external email or licensing request sent automatically.
- No source-text review packets released while the source-use gate is unresolved.
- No invented reviewer identity, review result, disagreement, or adjudication.
- No promotion into `data/gold`.
- No trading, account access, credentials, orders, custody, or KYC handling.

## Considered approaches

### Treat public access as permission

This is operationally simple but collapses two different facts: whether data can be fetched and whether it may be retained and used for proprietary research. The institutional notice makes that assumption especially weak. Rejected.

### Stop all work until permission arrives

This avoids accidental use, but it also prevents development and validation of controls that do not need real source text. It would leave the next human step underspecified. Rejected.

### Immutable evidence gate plus synthetic-tested review tooling

Capture hashes and minimal locators for official notices, write an explicit unresolved assessment, prepare a licensing-inquiry draft, and allow real source text into reviewer packets only after a separately supplied human approval covers the exact scope and corpus hashes. Exercise the allowed path only with synthetic fixtures until then. Selected.

## Architecture

```text
allowlisted official source pages
              |
              v
hash-only evidence capture + minimal excerpt/locator
              |
              v
source-use assessment -------------------------+
              |                                |
  requires_external_confirmation               | approved human record
              |                                |
              v                                v
metadata-only blocked inventory          blinded review packets
              |                                |
              +---------- no promotion --------+
                                               |
                                two independent reviews
                                               |
                                distinct adjudicator if needed
                                               |
                                  separate future promotion gate
```

Network evidence capture remains outside `polytrading.ai`. Review validation remains offline. Real intake files stay under ignored `var/` paths; committed files contain code, schemas, synthetic tests, and documentation only.

## Source evidence

An evidence request identifies:

- an allowlisted HTTPS URL;
- an explicit retrieval timestamp supplied by the operator;
- the expected source and evidence purpose;
- a short expected locator or excerpt used to detect an unexpected page.

The capture streams the response and records:

- final allowlisted URL, retrieval time, HTTP status, content type, byte count, and SHA-256;
- selected non-sensitive headers such as `ETag` and `Last-Modified` when present;
- a short relevant excerpt, capped at 25 words, and its SHA-256;
- a statement that the full response body was not retained.

Redirects, non-HTTPS URLs, non-200 responses, oversized bodies, unexpected content types, missing locators, and malformed UTF-8 fail closed. The full HTML document is neither written to disk nor logged.

Evidence proves what content was observed at a stated time. It does not prove acceptance, enforceability, or permission.

## Assessment and approval separation

`SourceUseAssessment` has one of three states:

- `requires_external_confirmation`: no source-text review or promotion;
- `rejected`: no source-text review or promotion;
- `approved`: usable only when accompanied by a valid, separately imported approval record.

Automated capture may create only `requires_external_confirmation`. It cannot elevate itself to `approved`.

A `SourceUseApproval` is supplied by a human-controlled process and binds:

- source and exact intended-use scope;
- evidence-record hashes;
- one or more intake-manifest hashes;
- approval basis/reference, effective time, and optional expiry;
- an identified approver and qualifying role, such as source-owner authorization or qualified legal review.

The implementation validates the shape, hashes, dates, and exact scope. It cannot establish that a claimed identity or legal qualification is genuine; that organizational control remains external. The repository supplies no command that invents or auto-signs an approval.

## Current intended-use scope

The scope is versioned and explicit:

- retain up to 1,000 public market records locally;
- create human semantic labels and derived aggregate statistics;
- use those labels for offline model evaluation and research for a proprietary automated-trading system;
- do not redistribute source text or offer a public dataset;
- do not train a generative model on source text in this phase.

Any material scope change requires a new assessment and approval. Approval for one manifest cannot authorize a later or different corpus implicitly.

## Review preparation

The queue builder consumes only completed, hash-verified intake runs. It always emits a decision report.

When the source-use decision is unresolved or rejected, it may emit a metadata-only blocked inventory containing candidate IDs, event-family IDs, source IDs, routing tags, and integrity hashes. It must not copy the question, description, resolution text, slug, public URL, or raw response into that inventory. It emits zero reviewer packets.

When a valid approval exactly covers the requested scope and every intake manifest, the builder may emit two blinded assignment packets:

- reviewer identifiers must be explicit, non-empty, and distinct;
- each packet contains stable item IDs, the exact input hash, the material needed to review, and the ontology version;
- neither packet contains the other reviewer's assignments or decisions;
- deterministic ordering and hashes make each packet reproducible;
- output directories must be new and outside `data/gold`.

Pseudonymous identifiers enforce separation in artifacts but do not prove that two different humans performed the work. That fact remains a human-process responsibility.

## Review and adjudication hardening

The existing corpus review commands gain an explicit corpus directory instead of hardcoding `data/gold`. On append they validate that:

- the item exists in the selected corpus;
- the supplied input hash equals the frozen item hash;
- reviewer role and identifier are allowed;
- the reviewer is assigned to the item when an assignment manifest is present;
- a reviewer cannot overwrite or duplicate a decision for the same item and role;
- adjudicators are distinct from both independent reviewers.

Two agreeing independent reviews complete an item. A disagreement requires exactly one distinct adjudication. Review appends remain separate from promotion.

## Licensing-inquiry draft

The real evidence run writes a ready-to-send text draft, but it does not send it. The draft identifies the intended scope, approximate record count, local retention, derived labels and evaluation, proprietary-research context, non-redistribution commitment, and corpus/evidence hashes. It asks Polymarket/ICE whether consultation or a license is required and what conditions apply.

No contact address is invoked by code. Sending the draft and evaluating any response are explicit human actions.

## Artifacts

A source-use evidence run under `var/source-use/<run-id>` contains:

- `evidence.jsonl`: minimal, hashed evidence records;
- `assessment.json`: unresolved machine assessment and exact intended-use scope;
- `licensing-inquiry.md`: unsent draft;
- `manifest.json`: inputs, file hashes, completion state, and schema versions.

A review-preparation run under `var/review-queue/<run-id>` contains:

- `decision.json`: gate inputs and allow/block reasons;
- `blocked_inventory.jsonl` when blocked;
- `reviewer-a/assignments.jsonl` and `reviewer-b/assignments.jsonl` only when allowed;
- `manifest.json`: deterministic artifact hashes and zero/nonzero packet counts.

The manifest is written last. Existing non-empty output directories are rejected. Partial runs cannot claim completion.

## Real-run expected result

For the current Polymarket open and closed cohorts:

- capture and hash the current official notices;
- record `requires_external_confirmation`;
- produce an unsent licensing-inquiry draft;
- produce a metadata-only inventory for all verified candidates;
- produce zero source-text reviewer packets and zero review records;
- leave `data/gold` unchanged.

That is a successful safety result, not a failed corpus build.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Tests prove automated evidence capture cannot issue an approval.
2. Tests cover URL allowlisting, body limits, content validation, hashing, locators, deterministic manifests, and partial-run behavior.
3. Synthetic fixtures prove exact-scope approval validation, expiry handling, manifest binding, and two blinded packet outputs.
4. Tests prove unresolved, rejected, expired, mismatched, and incomplete approvals emit zero source-text packets.
5. Blocked inventory tests prove source text and public URLs are absent.
6. Review append tests prove item existence, frozen input hashes, distinct reviewers, blinded assignments, and distinct adjudication.
7. A real official-source evidence run and review-preparation run produce the expected blocked result under `var/`.
8. `data/gold` remains byte-for-byte unchanged, the offline AI boundary remains intact, and the full test suite stays green.
